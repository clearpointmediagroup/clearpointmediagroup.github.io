How this works

This form is how you order a graphic from ClearPoint Media Group: a ready-to-post picture for a sale, special, event or announcement.

  1. Tell us what you want. Fill in this form. It takes 1 to 2 minutes.
  2. We make it. We email you a proof (a preview with a faint watermark) within 5 hours, 9 a.m. to 9 p.m.
  3. You look it over. OK it, or ask for changes (2 free).
  4. Pay only after you OK it, with a secure Square link. Your first graphic is free.
  5. Post it. You get the finished graphic for your phone, Facebook, Instagram or TV.

Why we ask: Your business info is asked one time, so we never ask again. The graphic part tells us what goes on your picture.

Your first graphic is free. After that, graphics are $25 each.
