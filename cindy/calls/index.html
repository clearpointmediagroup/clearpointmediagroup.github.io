How this page works
  1. Start at the top: call backs for today first, then new calls.
  2. Tap Call. When you hang up, you come right back here and the form is open.
  3. Tap what happened. Fill in the name and email if you got them. It saves as you type.
  4. Tap Save and close. "Got it" and "No thanks" leave your list. "No answer" and "Call back later" come back at the top on the call-back day.
  5. The practice card is a call to Randy. Saving it never removes it.
