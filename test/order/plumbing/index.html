About ClearPoint Media Group

We make professional graphics for local businesses, fast. Real estate agents use us for just listed, open house, price drop and just sold posts that look sharp on a phone, a big-screen TV, social media or a website. Every graphic is checked word for word before you see it, so names, prices and phone numbers are right. No contracts and no design work on your end. Just tell us what to say.

How this works

Get a ready-to-post graphic for your listing. Tap the kind of graphic you need, type a few words for each part, and add photos if you want, like the house, your headshot or your logo. Then pick where you'll show it: phone, big-screen TV, social media or your website. We design it and email you a preview to check. Most previews come back within 5 hours. Late-night and weekend orders are ready the next business morning. Look it over, ask for changes, and pay only once you OK it. $25 each, and your first one is free.
